Issue #11: macOS binary auto-detection is missing although releases ship darwin binaries
Severity: Medium
Component: Python SDK (python/dlengine.py)
Location: python/dlengine.py:114-147, .github/workflows/release.yml:46-48

Evidence:
  _auto_detect_binary branches on `platform.system() == "Windows"` and
  otherwise assumes Linux:

      if is_windows:
          candidates = [ ...windows... ]
      else:
          if "arm" in arch or "aarch64" in arch:
              candidates = [base_dir/"bin"/"dlengine-linux-arm64", ...]

  On macOS (platform.system() == "Darwin") it will look for
  `bin/dlengine-linux-amd64` or `bin/dlengine-linux-arm64`, neither of which
  is produced for macOS. The release workflow, however, builds
  dist/dlengine-darwin-amd64 and dist/dlengine-darwin-arm64.

Impact:
  DLEngine() raises FileNotFoundError on macOS out of the box, and the
  documented "Compatible with ... macOS" claim plus the darwin release
  artifacts are unusable through the SDK without manually passing bin_path.
